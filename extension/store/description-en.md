Sync your Mote bookmarks with Edge and Chrome — in both directions.

FEATURES
• Two-way sync — Bookmarks from Mote show up in your browser, and anything you add, edit or delete in the browser is synced back to Mote.
• Folders preserved — Mote folders become subfolders under "Bookmarks bar → Mote", so the hierarchy stays exactly as you organized it.
• Two ways to connect — Use the default Mote cloud account, or point the extension at your own WebDAV server.
• Two encryption modes — Standard mode derives its key from your account ID. With Advanced Data Protection enabled, data is end-to-end encrypted and can only be decrypted on your own devices with your password.
• Automatic or manual — Changes are pushed automatically after a short delay, and the cloud is checked at the interval you choose. Press "Sync now" whenever you want a manual sync.
• Scoped to one folder — Everything happens inside "Bookmarks bar → Mote". Your other browser bookmarks are never read or modified.

PRIVACY
• No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
• Credentials and settings stay in local extension storage.
• Data is sent only to your own sync service — the Mote cloud function or the WebDAV address you enter — never to third parties.
• No remote code: all logic ships inside the extension, and encryption uses the browser's built-in Web Crypto API.
